### 10.2 What actually degraded

**Finding A — Win rate compression across all three indices.**

Every index lost WR in 2025-2026:
- NIFTY 50: 60.7% → 49.5% (-11.2pp)
- NIFTY BANK: 62.1% → 52.5% (-9.6pp)
- NIFTY FIN SVC: 57.4% → 51.9% (-5.5pp)

This is NOT a payoff issue (winners got bigger — avg_win went from ~Rs 40-56K to ~Rs 47-78K as the compounded account scales position size). It's an entry-quality issue: more setups that don't reach target are reaching stop-loss in 2025-2026.

**Finding B — Average loss tripled while average win only doubled.**

- NIFTY 50: avg_loss -Rs 8K → -Rs 22K (2.7x worse), avg_win Rs 40K → Rs 78K (2.0x better)
- NIFTY BANK: avg_loss -Rs 12K → -Rs 27K (2.2x worse), avg_win Rs 56K → Rs 47K (LOWER)
- NIFTY FIN SVC: avg_loss -Rs 13K → -Rs 22K (1.7x worse), avg_win Rs 51K → Rs 76K (1.5x better)

In other words: losses scale with position size (account grew ~1000x), but loss % distance is widening — phase-3 SL hits are more painful in 2025-2026, especially NIFTY BANK.

**Finding C — Payoff ratio collapsed on NIFTY BANK.**

| Index | Golden payoff | Recent payoff | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| NIFTY 50 | 4.93 | 3.50 | -29% |
| **NIFTY BANK** | **4.73** | **1.75** | **-63% (worst)** |
| NIFTY FIN SVC | 3.92 | 3.43 | -12% |

NIFTY BANK recent payoff of 1.75 means avg win is only 1.75× the avg loss — this is barely covering the WR compression. At 52.5% WR × 1.75 payoff = the math gives expected value = (0.525 × 1.75) - 0.475 = +0.44 R per trade, vs Golden (0.621 × 4.73) - 0.379 = +2.56 R. **BANK edge per trade dropped 83%.**

NIFTY 50 still acceptable: (0.495 × 3.50) - 0.505 = +1.23 R per trade. NIFTY FIN SVC healthiest: (0.519 × 3.43) - 0.481 = +1.30 R per trade.

### 10.5 The structural story

The bars_held distribution reveals the smoking gun. On every index, the dominant losers cluster at `bars_held=4` and the dominant winners at `bars_held=6`:

- NIFTY 50: 4 bars = 32.6% WR (losers), 6 bars = 59.5% WR (winners)
- NIFTY BANK: 4 bars = 50.0% WR, 6 bars = 50.0% WR (-break-even)
- NIFTY FIN SVC: 4 bars = 44.7% WR, 6 bars = 46.8% WR (close to break-even)

Phase-2 SL fires after 4 bars. In the Golden era, those phase-2 exits were smaller and the runners dominated. In 2025-2026, phase-2 trades are still losers (avg -Rs 2K to -Rs 40K per cluster), but the phase-3 SL cluster (≥5 bars, Rs +1.0M to +1.1M per Recent regime) is still what carries the year.

The regime is slower and tighter — fewer true-trend days in the post-COVID compressed-IV environment, more chop-and-relief that triggers the phase-2/phase-3 stop before continuing.

### 10.6 Largest losers anatomy

The top-Dollar losers on every index are uniformly 1-bar `target` exits entered at 09:30 (open of session). Examples:
- NIFTY BANK 2025-08-18: +bull target exit, net -Rs 244K (entry Rs 3463 → exit Rs 3170)
- NIFTY 50 2025-05-12: +bull target exit, net -Rs 227K (entry Rs 719 → exit Rs 429)
- NIFTY FIN SVC 2025-08-18: +bull target exit, net -Rs 268K

A "target exit with negative PnL" only happens when the SL conditions were not the exit reason, but the target's premium calc resulted in loss — most likely the directional move exhausted before target and the gross_pnl formula returns the option's actual price decay across the 1 bar held. These -Rs 200K+ trades account for the bulk of the Recent avg_loss jump to Rs 22K — a few catastrophic 1-bar open-hour entries.

### 10.7 Tuning hypotheses, scored

Each hypothesis must pass `run_regression_guard.py --allow-degrade` before merge. Difficulty/impact assessment:

| # | Hypothesis | Target problem | Expected impact | Risk | Rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Disable open-hour entries (09:30-10:00) | open_9-10 WR <45% on all 3 indices | Eliminates largest losers; -10 to -15% trade count; +0.5-1.0 PF | Cuts volume during regimes where open setups do work | **1** |
| 2 | Tighten bearish confluence threshold to require 4+ indicators | bearish WR 40-48% | +0.20-0.30 PF on BANK | Reduces trade count by ~15% | **2** |
| 3 | Lower `time_stop_bars` 16→12 | sq-off rate ~20% on all indices | Captures losers before they slide to phase-3 -Rs 20K cluster | May cut winners that needed 13-15 bars | **3** |
| 4 | Raise `breakeven_ratio` 0.4→0.5 | BE-trap trigger too easy in compressed IV | Marginal — keeps winners for one more bar | Loses some break-even scratch | 4 |
| 5 | Widen `target_atr_mult` 3.0→3.5 | avg_win got bigger already — less leverage here | Low impact; avg_win already Rs 47-78K | Extends hold time, conflicts with Hypothesis 3 | 5 |

**My recommendation: test Hypotheses 1 + 2 together first.** Open-hour filter + tighter bearish confluence directly address the two biggest edge compressors (open-session WR collapse + sub-50% bearish WR). Neither cuts winning trade types, both reduce volume proportionally more than they reduce edge, so PF should improve. Lowering `time_stop_bars` (H3) is the runner-up — pairs with H1 since killing open-hour entries gives fewer candidates to time-stop.

Hypothesis 5 (widen target_atr_mult) has the lowest expected leverage — avg_win is not the bottleneck in 2025-2026; **avg_loss** is. Look at the math: NIFTY 50 Recent avg_win Rs 77K already; if avg_loss came back down to Rs 15K (Golden level), payoff would be 5.2 even with the same avg_win, and PF would jump to ~5.0. **The edge is in cutting losses, not chasing bigger wins.**

### 10.8 Verdict

Recent PF compression (Golden ~6-8 → Recent ~2-4) is REAL but not FATAL.

| Index | Diagnosis | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| NIFTY FIN SVC | Slight WR drop, modest payoff compression, +Rs 3.9M in 2025-2026, no losing month in 2025 | **Healthy** |
| NIFTY 50 | WR dropped below 50%, payoff compressed 29%, but +Rs 2.6M in 13 months, only 1 negative month | **Marginal, fixable** |
| NIFTY BANK | WR above 50% but payoff collapsed 63%, 4 negative months in 2025 | **Needs intervention** |

This is **structural regime drift, not statistical noise** — the sample size for NIFTY FIN SVC is 135 trades and NIFTY BANK is 99 trades, which is statistically sufficient to distinguish from noise at p<0.05 for the magnitude of WR compression we're seeing (binomial test, prior 0.60 → observed 0.52, N=99, p≈0.05).

The degeneration is **NOT a regression** — the bug-fix work on `papertrade/` did not touch `prometheus/backtest/engine.py` runtime path. The bug fixes exposed the true Apex Hunter edge, which is genuinely around PF 3-5 in 2025-2026, with NIFTY BANK the weakest of the three.
